What this desk does

Four readers, one decision.

A film and optional notes go to four frozen models. A small fusion model, trained on a public NIH subset, combines the three image readers. If they disagree, the study is referred instead of auto-reported.

Film + notes
Local texture
DenseNet-121 Global pattern
ViT-B/16 Localization
YOLOv8-World Clinical notes
CheXagent
Trained fusion
Agree: final diagnosis Disagree: refer, with maps

The long explanation, with algorithm diagrams, is in PROJECT.md in the project folder. Setup on another computer is in STEPS.md.
